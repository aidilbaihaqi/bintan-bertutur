About Bintan

Pulau Bintan is the largest island in Indonesia’s Riau Islands, famous for its beautiful beaches, clear waters, and rich Malay culture. Close to Singapore, it’s a popular destination for relaxing, water activities, and exploring nature, offering a peaceful island getaway.
